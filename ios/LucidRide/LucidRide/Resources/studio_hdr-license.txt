Picked for the "black warehouse with direct lights" cinematic aesthetic:
mostly-dark environment with a few small bright direct sources, creating
dramatic specular reflections on the bike's chrome and carpaint instead
of an even softbox fill. Used only as scene.lightingEnvironment.contents
— never rendered as the visible backdrop.
