Specifications:
- 567,213 triangles
- 28 meshes
- 22 named PBR materials (chrome, carpaint, glass, leather seat, tire tread,
  brake disc, calipers, etc.) with embedded texture maps
- 17.5 MB GLB binary

Chosen for the Lucid Ride aesthetic: a production-grade Ducati Super Sports
sport bike with full fairings, proper sport-bike silhouette, and PBR-correct
materials. Loaded through GLTFKit2 with the model's original geometry and
materials intact. The scene combines this with HDR image-based lighting
(Polyhaven Studio Small 04, CC0) to render at product-showcase quality.
