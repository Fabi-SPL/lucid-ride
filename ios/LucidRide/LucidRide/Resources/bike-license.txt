Chosen for the Lucid Ride aesthetic: a clean low-poly sport-bike silhouette
with proper fairings, swept tail, and racing posture — runtime-recoloured to
match the Lucid logo (pure black-and-white monochrome on a black background,
no chroma accents). The low-poly geometry is intentional and read as
"logo-style" rather than photorealistic.

The model is shipped unmodified in geometry. Material colour overrides are
applied at scene-load time to enforce the monochrome look.
