3D Model: Suzuki SV650 (low-poly sport bike)

Chosen for the Lucid Ride aesthetic: a clean low-poly sport-bike silhouette
with proper fairings, swept tail, and racing posture. The model ships with
its original PBR materials intact — the renderer combines them with HDR
image-based lighting (Polyhaven Studio Small 04, CC0) to produce a premium
product-showcase look despite the low triangle count.

The model is shipped unmodified in geometry and materials. Only the
headlight material's emission is boosted at scene-load so the lamp reads
as "powered on" against the dark background.
